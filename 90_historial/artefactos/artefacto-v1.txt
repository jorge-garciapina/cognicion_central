1) CORE DEFINITION (GENERAL)
───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
Definition (General):
An ARTIFACT is an intentionally constructed object produced by a process, with a
stable contract (format + interpretation rules), that encapsulates logic or evidence
in a way that can be transported, inspected, verified, and used as input/output in
other processes. ESTA DEFINICIÓN PARTE DE LA IDEA  QUE EXPONES EN LA SECCIÓN 0) WHAT THIS DOCUMENT IS Y QUE YA CORREGÍ, LA IDEA NO ES TRANSPORTAR, LA IDEA ES CREAR "FOTOGRAFÍAS COGNITIVAS"

Key points:
- “Intentionally constructed”: it exists because someone designed a procedure to produce it. ESTO ES OBVIO, Y DE HECHO ES EQUIVOCADO, CONSIDERA QUE LOS ARTEFACTOS SON CONSTRUIDOS USANDO LA IA, SÓLO RECUERDA EL PROCEDIMIENTO QUE HEMOS IMPLEMENTADO PARA CONSTRUIRLOS EN OTROS CHATS DE ESTE PROYECTO
- “Stable contract”: others can read it without guessing what it means. ESTO ES FALSO, LA IDEA ES JUSTO LA OPUESTA: NO BUSCO QUE OTROS HUMANOS PUEDAN LEERLO, BUSCO CREAR UNA FOTOGRAFÍA COGNITIVA OPTIMIZADA PARA QUE LA IA PUEDA ENTENDERLA Y USARLA, RECUERDA: EL PROBLEMA CON LOS HUMANOS ES QUE ES IMPOSIBLE COMPRENDER ALGO TAN GRANDE COMO UN PROYECTO DE CÓDIGO, SI AFIRMAS QUE LA IDEA ES QUE "OTROS PUEDAN LEERLO SIN ADIVINAR QUÉ ES", ENTONCES NO HAS COMPRENDIDO BIEN EL CONCEPTO DETRÁS, ES DECIR, QUE PROPORCIONEN AL HUMANO CAPACIDADES CON LAS QUE NO SE CUENTA
- “Encapsulates logic or evidence”: it carries rules, structure, or factual traces of a system. AUNQUE ESTO ES VERDAD NO ES LO QUE ME IMPORTA CON LOS ARTEFACTOS: LO QUE BUSCO ES ENVIAR ALGO "EN CRUDO" (RECUERDA EL EJEMPLO DE CÓDIGO, LO ÚNICO QUE HAGO ES PONER LOS ARCHIVOS COMO UN ARCHIVO JSONL CON PATH Y CONTENT, EN NINGÚN MOMENTO ME DETENGO A PENSAR EN LA EVIDENCIA, ESTRUCTURA NI NINGUNA COSA QUE DICES, ES LA LABOR DE LA IA DETECTAR ESAS COSAS Y AYUDAR AL HUMANO A COMPRENDERLAS). 
- “Portable”: it moves between contexts (tools, people, environments) without losing identity. DE NUEVO, ESTA NO ES LA IDEA, LA IDEA ES CREAR UNA FOTOGRAFÍA COGNITIVA QUE LA IA PODRÁ UTILIZAR PARA AYUDAR AL HUMANO A COMPRENDER LA ESTRUCTURA DETRÁS DE UN PROYECTO CON MUCHOS ELEMENTOS "ACESIBLES A LA IA" (CON "ACCESIBLES A LA IA" ME REFIERO, POR EJEMPLO A CÓDIGO, O LENGUAJE QUE LA IA PUEDE COMPRENDER)
- “Operable”: it enables actions (analysis, reconstruction, transformation, validation).

SOBRE ESTOS EJEMPLOS, QUIERO QUE LOS ELIMINES Y TE ENFOQUES ÚNICAMENTE EL EJEMPLO DE CÓDIGO QUE YA ANALIZAMOS, ME DOY CUENTA QUE

An ARTIFACT is a portable, verifiable snapshot of a system (or subsystem) that is

E) Transformation (apply patches/refactors while preserving invariants)

3) THE MINIMAL INVARIANTS OF AN ARTIFACT
───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
To qualify as an artifact in the strong sense, it must have:

(1) IDENTITY (Address)
A stable way to say:
- “What unit is this?”
- “Where does it live in the system?”
Examples of address formats:
- path-like: src/server.ts
- semantic: policy/access-control/rule-17
- hierarchical: workflow/claims/intake/node-3
- indexed: dataset/orders/2026-01/partition-004

(2) CONTENT (Content)
Interpretable payload:
- code, configuration, rules, schemas, logs, traces, decisions, narratives, etc.

(3) CONTRACT (Schema + Rules) ESTO NO APLICA
Explicit rules for interpretation:
- fields, types, encoding
- how to parse
- how to detect missing parts
- what is excluded by design

(4) PROVENANCE (Provenance) ESTO NO APLICA
Traceability: you can answer:
- How was it produced?
- From what sources?
- Under what exclusions?
- At what time/version?
- With what completeness limitations?

(5) INTEGRITY SIGNALS (Integrity) ESTO NO APLICA
Mechanisms to verify the artifact has not been corrupted or silently modified:
- hashes, checksums, signatures, counts, deterministic ordering

Without these invariants, you can still have “a file”, but not an artifact in the
engineering sense. ESTA AFIRMACIÓN DEJA DE SER CORRECTA SI CONSIDERAMOS TODAS LAS CORRECCIONES QUE HE HECHO HASTA ESTE PUNTO

ARTEFACTO Y AÑADO ESE ARTEFACTO EN FORMA DE DOCUMENTO POR EJEMPLO, A UN PROYECTO DE CHATGPT, ENTONCES SERÁ POSIBLE PROVEER A

ARQUITECTURA. 
ESTE PUNTO ES MUY IMPORTANTE, NO LO PASES POR ALTO

SI CONSIDERAS LO QUE DIGO, TE DARÁS CUENTA DE QUE LO QUE DICES EN ESTA SECCIÓN ES IRRELEVANTE AL VERDADERO OBJETIVO QUE TENGO

───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
Artifacts are a solution to a structural problem:

Problem:
A system is larger than what can be safely or conveniently transferred in its native form
(e.g., full repository, full database, full operational context, secrets, binaries, environment).

Artifact solution:
Instead of transferring the full system, you transfer a structured snapshot that preserves:
- architecture (what exists and how it is organized)
- constraints (what must not be broken)
- contracts (inputs/outputs)
- evidence (what actually happened, if you include execution artifacts)
- style conventions (how changes should look)

Artifacts enable:
- Collaborative understanding without full access.
- Deterministic iteration: “new snapshot → analysis → patch → new snapshot”.
- Auditing and compliance: “prove what was present and what was excluded”.
- Rapid onboarding: “this is the system, summarized but reconstructable”.
- Safe sharing: exclude secrets and sensitive data by design.

Artifacts convert a complex system into a portable “object of reasoning”. LO QUE DICES “object of reasoning” ESTÁ BIEN, QUIZÁS ES LO ÚNICO RESCATABLE

5) MAIN TYPES OF ARTIFACTS (A TAXONOMY) COMO YA DIJE, POR EL MOMENTO SÓLO DEJA EL EJEMPLO DE CÓDIGO QUE HEMOS TRABAJADO, ELIMINA TODOS LOS DEMÁS. 
───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
5.1 Code Project Artifact (Source Snapshot)
Purpose:
- Capture the architecture and logic of a “code project” (not limited to source code:
  can include configuration and documentation).
Typical content:
- source files, configs, docs, tests, schemas
Typical operations:
- analyze architecture, generate patches, reconstruct project, compare versions

5.2 System Artifact (Multi-layer Snapshot)
Purpose:
- Capture code + contracts + decisions + operational instructions.
Adds:
- requirements/specifications, Architectural Decision Records (ADR), runbooks, policies

5.3 Data Artifact (Dataset Snapshot / Sample)
Purpose:
- Capture data shapes and representative samples to validate assumptions and reproduce issues.
Adds:
- schema, sampling method, anonymization method, partitioning rules

5.4 Execution Artifact (Runtime Evidence)
Purpose:
- Capture what actually happened during system execution:
  structured logs, traces, business events, real error cases, performance profiles.
Adds:
- correlation IDs, timestamps, sanitized payloads, environment signals (non-secret)

5.5 Research Artifact (Theory/Model Snapshot)
Purpose:
- Capture a model, its assumptions, and its derivations in a portable object:
  equations, definitions, references, experiments, results.

All of these share the same core invariants:
Address + Content + Contract + Provenance + Integrity.

6) KEY CONCEPT: “ADDRESS + CONTENT” (GENERALIZED PATH + SCRIPT) ME GUSTA ESTA SECCIÓN 
───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
In a code project, you typically have:
- path: “where the file lives”
- script/content: “the file contents”

Generalization:
- Address: a stable identifier that locates a unit inside the system
- Content: the interpretable payload that defines the unit’s logic or evidence

This generalization is what allows “logic snapshots” for ANY process, not only code.
Example:
- Address: workflow/sales/lead-qualification/step-2
- Content: YAML describing decision rules for that step

This is the conceptual bridge that makes artifacts universal.

7) A CANONICAL SCHEMA FOR ARTIFACT LINES (JSONL / NDJSON STYLE) ES IMPORTANTE QUE ACLARES QUE TODO ESTO ES PARTE DEL EJEMPLO DE 
CÓDIGO, DEBIDO A QUE LO PONES COMO UNA SECCIÓN APARTE DA LA IMPRESIÓN DE QUE TODOS LOS ARTEFACTOS QUE PUEDAN CREARSE DEBEN 
SEGUIR ESTA CONFIGURACIÓN LO CUAL NO ES VERDAD, ESTA CONFIGURACIÓN ES VÁLIDA PARA EL EJEMPLO DE CÓDIGO, NO DIGO QUE ESTÉ MAL 
LO QUE DESCRIBES EN ESTA SECCIÓN, SÓLO DIGO QUE DEBES PONERLO COMO PARTE DEL EJEMPLO DE CÓDIGO, NO COMO UNA SECCIÓN DE LA DEFINICIÓN 
GENERAL DE ARTEFACTO (ESTE CODE SNIPPET)
───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
A widely useful representation is a line-oriented artifact:
one unit per line, each line a JSON object.

Minimal schema:
{
  "address": "string",
  "kind": "text|binary",
  "content": "string (if kind=text)"
}

Common extensions (optional but recommended):
{
  "address": "src/server.ts",
  "kind": "text",
  "content": "...",
  "sha256": "hex-string",
  "size": 12345,
  "encoding": "utf-8",
  "mtime": "2026-01-10T12:00:00Z",
  "tags": ["code", "server", "http"]
}

Notes:
- “address” can be “path” for file-based projects.
- “binary” entries often omit content to avoid huge payloads or sensitive data.
- metadata enables integrity checks and auditing.

Why line-based?
- Streaming and partial processing
- Easy diffs and chunking
- Robust to large collections

9) NON-EXAMPLES (WHAT IS NOT AN ARTIFACT IN THIS STRONG SENSE) ESTO NO ES NECESARIO, PRIMERO DEBEMOS TENER UNA DEFINICIÓN CORRECTA
───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
9.1 “A random text file” with no schema, no provenance, no identity rules.
- If nobody can interpret it consistently, it is not an artifact; it is just text.

9.2 “A zip of a repo with secrets included.”
- It might be portable, but it violates safe sharing. Artifacts require governance.

9.3 “A screenshot of code.”
- It is evidence, but not operable: hard to diff, reconstruct, patch, or validate.

9.4 “A folder of files with no stable naming and no contract.”
- Without identity rules and interpretation, collaboration becomes guesswork.

Artifacts are defined by CONTRACT + OPERABILITY, not by being “a file”.

11) GOVERNANCE: SECRETS, SENSITIVE DATA, AND SAFETY ESTO NO APLICA, ES ALGO QUE SE RELACIONA CON EL EJEMPLO DE ARTEFACTO DE CÓDIGO 
QUE HEMOS ESTADO ESTUDIANDO, NO APLICARÁ PARA TODOS LOS CASOS POSIBLES EN LOS QUE PODAMOS CREAR UN ARTEFACTO 
───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
A safe artifact is designed to be shareable.

Rules:
- Exclude secrets by default (e.g., .env, keys, tokens, credentials).
- If examples are needed, use placeholders (e.g., ACCOUNT_SID=ACxxxx).
- Anonymize personal data (hashing, redaction, generalization).
- Avoid embedding private user content unless explicitly necessary.
- Provide explicit “exclusions” in provenance.

Artifacts should reduce risk, not amplify it.

───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
12) COMPLETENESS AND LIMITATIONS (TRUTHFULNESS OF THE SNAPSHOT) ESTO NO APLICA
───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
Every artifact must be honest about scope.
Include:
- what is included
- what is excluded
- whether any content is truncated
- which version/time it corresponds to
- whether it is “full snapshot” or “focused snapshot”

A partial artifact is acceptable if it is explicitly partial.

14) THE ONE-SENTENCE DEFINITION (FOR QUICK REUSE) ES NECESARIO QUE REVISES ESTA DEFINICIÓN, HAY MUCHOS COMENTARIOS QUE 
INVALIDAN PARTES, POR EJEMPLO governed by an explicit contract (Schema/Rules) and provenance, EL ARTEFACTO NO ESTÁ "GOVERNADO"
POR NADA, YA QUE SU INTENCIÓN ES CREAR UNA FOTOGRAFÍA COGNITIVA DE LA LÓGICA EN CRUDO, SIN REFINAR, EL PUNTO ES JUSTO ESE: LA IA 
COMPRENDE ESA LÓGICA EN CRUDO Y LA HACE ACCESIBLE AL HUMANO 
───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
An artifact is a portable, verifiable snapshot of a system’s logic or evidence,
made of addressable units (Address) with interpretable content (Content), 
governed by an explicit contract (Schema/Rules) and provenance, so it can be analyzed,
compared, reconstructed, and safely transformed without transferring the entire system.

15) QUICK CHECKLIST: “IS THIS REALLY AN ARTIFACT?” ESTO NO ES IMPORTANTE 
───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
Answer YES to all:
[ ] Does each unit have a stable identity (address)?
[ ] Is the content interpretable (text or structured data)?
[ ] Is there a clear schema/contract for reading it?
